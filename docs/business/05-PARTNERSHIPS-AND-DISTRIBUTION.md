# Partnerships and Distribution

## Principle

Partnerships and distribution are structural components of the business model.

[COMPANY_NAME] should remain the customer's strategic architect and accountable technology partner while using qualified external organizations for distribution, specialist delivery, independent review, regulated work, and geographic coverage.

## Microsoft partner pathway

Potential purposes:

- Credibility
- Technical enablement
- Co-selling
- Marketplace listings
- Partner incentives
- Managed-service capabilities
- Security capabilities
- Customer licensing and administration

No specific designation or sequence is approved. Current requirements must be researched before execution.

## Distributor / CSP model

The company may use an authorized cloud distributor rather than constructing independent billing and provisioning systems.

Desired capabilities:

- Subscription provisioning
- Consolidated billing
- License-management tools
- Technical escalation
- Partner enablement
- Adjacent cloud and security catalog
- Financing and payment support
- Marketplace access
- Automation and integration interfaces
- Clear support and incident ownership

Pax8 was discussed historically as one option. The architecture remains distributor-neutral.

## Independent attestation model

Example:

1. [COMPANY_NAME] performs readiness, control design, evidence organization, and remediation.
2. A qualified independent firm performs the formal examination or attestation.
3. [COMPANY_NAME] helps maintain and improve the environment after the engagement.

## Referral and channel categories

- Accounting firms
- Legal firms
- Insurance brokers
- Investors and private-equity groups
- Business consultants
- Software vendors
- Telecom providers
- MSPs and specialists

## Partner governance requirements

Every material partner relationship should define:

- Role and scope
- Qualification criteria
- Customer ownership
- Contracting party
- Pricing and referral economics
- Confidentiality and data handling
- Security requirements
- Insurance
- Liability and indemnification
- Support and escalation
- Quality measures
- Conflict and independence requirements
- Termination and transition
- Periodic review
