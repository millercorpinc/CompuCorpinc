# Business Plan

## Document status

This is the formal working business plan for `[COMPANY_NAME]`.

It consolidates the most complete business model developed in the Startup project. It is authoritative for planning and execution unless replaced by an approved decision record, executed agreement, or later canonical document.

The final company name, actual owners and participants, legal structure, role assignments, compensation, and authority remain unresolved.

## 1. Executive summary

`[COMPANY_NAME]` is a principal-led technology advisory, implementation, security, automation, and managed-governance company serving organizations that need enterprise-quality technology judgment without building a complete internal technology department.

The company’s operating formula is:

> Advisory at the front, standardized implementation in the middle, and recurring governance at the back.

The company begins by understanding the customer’s business, risks, current technology, ownership gaps, and operational constraints. It then designs and implements a secure, supportable baseline. Finally, it provides continuing governance, security review, change management, roadmap ownership, automation maintenance, and selective managed support.

Microsoft is the principal specialization and commercial anchor. The company is not Microsoft-only. It integrates Microsoft cloud, workplace, identity, endpoint, security, collaboration, data, and automation capabilities with the customer’s wider application and vendor environment.

The initial core market is approximately 10–150 employees, with a broader practical range of roughly 1–200 users. The strongest initial offers are a paid technology/security assessment and the Secure Microsoft 365 Foundation, followed by recurring Microsoft Security and Governance.

## 2. Customer problem

Small and lower-midmarket organizations frequently have substantial technology dependence but limited senior technology leadership.

Common conditions include:

- Microsoft 365 deployed without coherent governance
- Weak or inconsistent identity, device, collaboration, and data controls
- One internal generalist or an incumbent provider focused mainly on tickets
- Unclear administrative ownership and excessive privileged access
- Incomplete documentation
- Manual administrative and financial workflows
- Multiple disconnected SaaS and line-of-business systems
- Increasing cyber-insurance, customer, contractual, or compliance requirements
- Vendor sprawl and poor technology investment sequencing
- No clear roadmap connecting business priorities to technology work

These organizations do not merely need another software product. They need accountable judgment, standards, implementation discipline, and continuing ownership.

## 3. Solution and value proposition

`[COMPANY_NAME]` gives customers a senior technology operating capability that can:

- Establish a clear strategy and target architecture
- Secure and govern Microsoft 365 and related systems
- Standardize identity, endpoints, collaboration, and administration
- Connect fragmented applications and information flows
- Automate high-friction work
- Prepare technology controls and evidence for compliance requirements
- Coordinate specialist and regulated partners
- Maintain documentation and operating ownership over time

Working value proposition:

> We design secure, governable technology environments that owners can actually run.

Working delivery promise:

> Enterprise precision, small-team speed.

These phrases are working language, not approved brand copy.

## 4. Market definition

### Typical buyers

- Owner or founder
- President

### Qualification characteristics

A strong-fit customer usually has several of the following:

- No experienced CIO, CTO, security leader, or cloud architect

## 5. Competitive position

The company is positioned between several imperfect alternatives:

- Commodity MSPs that optimize for ticket volume and standard bundles
- Independent consultants without implementation or continuing ownership
- Software resellers focused primarily on licenses
- Large systems integrators that are too expensive or heavy for the customer
- Internal generalists without senior architecture, security, or transformation capacity

The differentiation is the combination of:

- Senior advisory and architecture
- Standardized implementation
- Security and maintainability by design
- Microsoft depth with ecosystem flexibility
- Practical integration and automation
- Strong documentation and evidence
- Recurring governance and accountability
- Coordinated use of qualified partners

## 6. Service portfolio

### 6.1 Technology, security, and governance assessment

A paid entry engagement that establishes:

- Current-state inventory
- Business and risk context
- Identity, endpoint, collaboration, security, data, and administration gaps
- Ownership and documentation gaps
- Target-state recommendations
- Prioritized roadmap
- Implementation options and estimates

This is the preferred entry point when the environment is inherited, poorly documented, or difficult to bound.

### 6.2 Secure Microsoft 365 Foundation

The primary productized implementation offer.

Two variants are maintained:

#### Foundation Core

- Primarily for fewer than 10 users
- Estimated 26–32 delivery hours
- Working price range of $6,000–$9,000
- Security and administration essentials with reduced advanced tuning and documentation depth

#### Full Foundation

- Generally for 10 or more users or customers needing the complete baseline
- Base estimate of 46 hours for a cooperative, controlled environment
- Working price range of $12,000–$18,000 before complexity adjustments
- Complete identity, privileged access, Intune, endpoint security, email security, Secure Score, data, retention, logging, documentation, quality assurance, and handoff scope

Detailed product requirements live in `docs/services/01-SECURE-WORKPLACE-FOUNDATION.md`.

## 7. Revenue model

The revenue architecture has four connected layers:

1. Paid assessments and Foundation projects
2. Implementation, remediation, migration, integration, and automation projects
3. Recurring advisory, security, governance, and managed-service revenue
4. Supplemental licensing and partner-related revenue

### Microsoft commercial structure

Microsoft-oriented engagements should separate:

1. Project fees
2. License pass-through, generally at Microsoft list price through an indirect-CSP/distributor pathway
3. Ongoing security and governance fees

Licensing margin is supplemental. It is not the core value proposition.

### Historical recurring hypotheses

- Governance retainers: approximately $500–$2,000+ monthly
- Lighter governance: approximately $5–$15 per user monthly
- Broader managed bundles: approximately $75–$100, $125–$150, and $175+ per user
- Fractional advisory retainers: approximately $2,000–$10,000 monthly

These ranges require cost, capacity, margin, and pilot validation before publication.

The company requires the following operating functions:
