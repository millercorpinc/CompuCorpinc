# Business Definition

## Purpose

Define what the company is, what it is not, and how it creates value.

## Definition

[COMPANY_NAME] is a founder-led technology advisory, implementation, security, automation, and managed-governance company.

It helps organizations establish and operate secure, integrated, supportable technology environments through senior guidance, standardized delivery, practical automation, and recurring accountability.

## Operating formula

> Advisory at the front, standardized implementation in the middle, and recurring governance at the back.

## Primary specialization

Microsoft cloud, workplace, identity, endpoint, collaboration, security, data-governance, automation, and related capabilities.

## Ecosystem position

The company is vendor-flexible across the client's business systems. It selects and connects the simplest supportable combination of platforms, integrations, scripts, workflows, and partners needed to achieve the outcome.

## The company is

- A fractional technology leadership capability
- A secure workplace and cloud architecture partner
- An implementation and remediation team
- A systems integration and automation partner
- A security and compliance-readiness partner
- A managed governance provider
- A coordinator of qualified partners and distributors

## The company is not primarily

- A commodity help desk
- A break/fix shop
- A staffing firm
- A license-markup business
- A full-scale global systems integrator
- An independent audit or attestation firm
- A custom-software factory without recurring operational ownership
