# Go-to-Market

## Initial motion

- Founder-led selling
- Warm professional network
- Former clients and colleagues
- LinkedIn outreach
- Focused website and one-pager
- Direct discovery and proposals
- Pilot clients
- Testimonials, referrals, and case studies

## Entry-offer candidates

1. Technology, security, and governance assessment
2. Secure workplace foundation
3. Fractional technology roadmap
4. Integration and automation opportunity assessment
5. Virtual workspace assessment or implementation
6. Compliance-readiness assessment

## Sales method

Lead with a specific business condition and outcome rather than a broad claim about digital transformation.

Example structure:

- Observed operational problem
- Business impact
- Existing-system opportunity
- Proposed assessment or implementation
- Measurable outcome
- Clear next step

## Solution-opportunity catalog

The proposed catalog should help:

- Rank opportunities by impact and effort
- Prepare targeted outreach
- Improve discovery
- Reuse solution patterns
- Generate proposals
- Build case studies
- Identify recurring attach opportunities

Use `templates/SOLUTION-OPPORTUNITY.md`.

## Evidence loop

Every pilot should capture:

- Starting condition
- Scope and assumptions
- Time and cost
- Outcome
- Customer feedback
- Reusable assets
- Delivery risks
- Pricing lessons
- Expansion opportunities
