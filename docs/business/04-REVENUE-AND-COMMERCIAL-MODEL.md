# Revenue and Commercial Model

## Revenue layers

1. Assessments and foundation projects
2. Implementation and remediation projects
3. Recurring advisory, governance, security, and managed services
4. Licensing and partner-related revenue

## Commercial principles

- Professional judgment and accountability are the main value.
- Licensing simplifies the relationship but is not the primary margin engine.
- Fixed-fee work requires explicit assumptions and change control.
- Recurring services require clear boundaries, service hours, and escalation rules.
- Standardized work should become more predictable over time.
- Custom work must not silently enter a standard package.
- Pricing must reflect delivery labor, overhead, partner costs, risk, rework, support burden, and desired margin.

## Historical hypotheses

Retained for validation only:

- Governance retainers: $500–$2,000+ monthly
- Light governance: $5–$15 per user monthly
- Broader bundles:
  - Core: $75–$100 per user
  - Pro: $125–$150 per user
  - Enterprise: $175+ per user
- General consulting projects: $3,000–$15,000
- Broader advisory retainers: $2,000–$10,000 monthly

## Required commercial models

Before launch, create:

- Service cost model
- Capacity model
- Gross-margin model
- Cash-flow forecast
- Startup-capital plan
- License pass-through policy
- Partner/subcontractor cost policy
- Discount and approval policy
- Change-order process
- Collections and suspension policy
