# Business Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Founder alignment

- Name process
- Founder roles
- Ownership and governance
- Risk tolerance
- Capital and compensation
- Initial customer and offer
- Decision-making process

## Phase 1 — Legal and operational foundation

- Entity and tax advice
- Formation and registrations
- Banking and accounting
- Insurance
- Contracts
- Internal security baseline
- Core operating tools
- Partner and distributor research

## Phase 2 — Commercial readiness

- Initial service package
- Pricing model
- Sales narrative
- Website and one-pager
- Discovery and proposal process
- Pilot delivery plan
- Partner agreements where needed

## Phase 3 — Pilot delivery

- Warm outreach
- Paid pilots
- Delivery evidence
- Case studies
- Refined scope and pricing
- Recurring conversion

## Phase 4 — Standardization

- PSA and documentation
- Service standards
- Automation and templates
- Knowledge system
- Metrics
- Quality and risk reviews

## Phase 5 — Scaled growth

- Partner-led acquisition
- Distributor optimization
- Specialist capacity
- Packaged vertical solutions
- Marketplace where justified
- Hiring and management systems
