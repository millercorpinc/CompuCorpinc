# Fractional Technology Leadership

## Status

Core recurring advisory service.

## Ideal customer

An organization that needs senior technology judgment and accountability but does not require or cannot justify a full-time executive technology leader.

## Outcomes

- Clear technology strategy
- Prioritized roadmap
- Better vendor and investment decisions
- Security and risk oversight
- Executive reporting
- Coordinated delivery
- Reduced dependence on informal knowledge

## Standard responsibilities

- Executive technology advisory
- Roadmap and budget
- Vendor and platform strategy
- Security and risk prioritization
- Policy and governance
- Architecture review
- Project portfolio oversight
- Partner coordination
- Quarterly or monthly operating reviews

## Boundaries

The service does not automatically include unlimited implementation, end-user support, formal audit, legal advice, or round-the-clock incident response.

## Cadence

Possible structures:

- Monthly executive advisory
- Biweekly operating review
- Quarterly roadmap review
- Defined availability for decisions and escalations

## Deliverables

- Technology roadmap
- Decision log
- Risk register
- Budget and investment plan
- Vendor and licensing review
- Executive reporting
- Project priorities

## Commercial hypothesis

Historical working range: approximately $2,000–$10,000 monthly, depending on organization size, meeting cadence, project oversight, availability, and risk ownership.
