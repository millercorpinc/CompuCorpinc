# Technology, Security, and Governance Assessment

## Status

Presumptive entry offer for uncertain or inherited environments.

## Ideal customer

An organization that knows its technology environment is fragmented, insecure, undocumented, expensive, or difficult to operate but cannot yet define the right implementation project.

## Outcomes

- Clear current-state understanding
- Material risks and gaps
- Prioritized remediation roadmap
- Target operating model
- Recommended service and partner path
- Budget and implementation sequence

## Assessment domains

- Business priorities and operating constraints
- Identity and access
- Endpoint and device management
- Collaboration and information structure
- Security and logging
- Data governance
- Business applications and integrations
- Network and remote access
- Vendor and licensing position
- Documentation and operational ownership
- Compliance and contractual requirements
- Backup, recovery, and continuity

## Deliverables

- Executive summary
- Current-state architecture
- Risk and gap register
- Priority matrix
- Target-state recommendations
- 90-day remediation plan
- Longer-term roadmap
- Budgetary estimate
- Proposed implementation scope

## Boundaries

The assessment does not constitute a formal audit, penetration test, legal opinion, or compliance attestation unless separately contracted through qualified parties.

## Commercial model

Use a paid fixed-fee assessment when the environment can be reasonably bounded. Use a discovery phase or time-and-materials structure when access, inventory, or scope is uncertain.

## Expansion path

- Secure workplace foundation
- Security remediation
- Virtual workspace
- Integration and automation
- Fractional technology leadership
- Managed governance
