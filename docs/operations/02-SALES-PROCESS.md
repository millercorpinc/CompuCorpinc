# Sales Process

## Principle

Sell a specific business outcome and operating improvement, not a generic promise of digital transformation.

## Stages

### 1. Target

Identify organizations with a likely trigger, buyer, and fit.

### 2. Connect

Use trusted relationships, referrals, focused outreach, or partner introductions.

### 3. Discover

Understand:

- Business condition
- Desired outcome
- Current systems and ownership
- Risk and urgency
- Internal capabilities
- Decision process
- Timeline and budget context

### 4. Qualify

A qualified opportunity has:

- A real business problem
- A responsible buyer or sponsor
- A feasible environment
- A defined next decision
- A plausible budget and timing path
- Acceptable legal and security risk

### 5. Shape

Develop the recommended assessment, service, implementation, or advisory path.

### 6. Propose

State:

- Outcome
- Scope
- Exclusions
- Assumptions
- Customer responsibilities
- Deliverables
- Acceptance
- Price and payment
- Change process
- Recurring implications

### 7. Contract and onboard

Confirm access, stakeholders, payment, schedule, and risk controls.

### 8. Expand

Use delivery evidence and operating reviews to identify recurring governance and additional improvements.

## Disqualification signals

- No accountable sponsor
- Expectation of unlimited support without adequate price
- Refusal to meet security prerequisites
- Unclear or unacceptable payment risk
- Unsupported legacy environment without funded discovery
- Request for formal professional work the company is not qualified to perform
- Customer demands that create unethical, illegal, or unmanageable risk
