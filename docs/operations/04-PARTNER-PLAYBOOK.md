# Partner and Distributor Playbook

## Partner types

- Technology vendor
- Distributor / indirect CSP
- Referral partner
- Subcontractor
- Specialist delivery partner
- Independent professional or attestation partner
- Strategic alliance

## Qualification

Evaluate:

- Capability and customer fit
- Credentials and independence
- References and evidence
- Security and data handling
- Insurance
- Geographic coverage
- Capacity and responsiveness
- Commercial model
- Contract terms
- Support and escalation
- Customer ownership
- Continuity and concentration risk

## Distributor evaluation

Compare:

- Microsoft licensing capability
- Product catalog
- Provisioning and billing
- Tax support
- Credit terms
- Technical support
- Partner enablement
- API and automation
- Marketplace options
- Contract and termination
- Security and data handling
- Economics

Do not select solely on margin.

## Engagement rules

Before involving a partner:

- Define customer-facing role
- Define contracting party
- Define scope and acceptance
- Define access and data handling
- Define communication and escalation
- Define intellectual-property ownership
- Define pricing, referral fees, and payment
- Confirm insurance and confidentiality

## Review

Review critical partners after each early engagement and at least annually thereafter.
