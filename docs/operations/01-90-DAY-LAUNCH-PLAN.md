# 90-Day Launch Plan

## Objective

Establish `[COMPANY_NAME]` as a legally and operationally viable business, approve the initial productized service sequence, complete the critical partner and security foundations, and secure the first paid pilot customer.

Historical named-person assignments are not active. The first launch step is to identify current participants and assign required functions.

## Days 1–30 — Formalize and prepare

### Participation, roles, and legal

- Identify actual owners and launch participants
- Assign commercial, technology/security/strategy, delivery/operations, finance/risk, and partner functions
- Document interim decision and signature authority
- Finalize company name
- Obtain legal and tax advice
- Decide whether the historical single-member LLC direction remains appropriate
- Form the entity
- Establish ownership, IP, confidentiality, authority, and departure documents

### Finance and risk

- Obtain EIN and required registrations
- Open business banking
- Establish accounting, expense, approval, and reconciliation controls
- Build a current startup-cost, cash-reserve, and 18-month forecast
- Obtain initial insurance
- Prepare MSA, SOW, NDA, partner, and subcontractor templates
- Approve deposit, milestone billing, payment, suspension, and change-order policies

### Commercial

- Approve the initial target customer
- Approve the assessment-to-Foundation launch sequence
- Confirm Foundation Core qualification and scope
- Confirm Full Foundation qualification and scope
- Build cost, capacity, price, margin, and contingency models from:
  - Foundation Core: 26–32-hour baseline and $6,000–$9,000 working range
  - Full Foundation: 46-hour controlled-environment baseline and $12,000–$18,000 working range
- Define recurring Microsoft Security and Governance scope and minimum fee

### Technology

- Establish secure company identity and endpoints
- Establish collaboration and document management
- Establish secret management and source control
- Define customer separation and least-privileged access
- Select minimal CRM, quoting, project/service management, documentation, finance, and reporting capabilities
- Establish repository ownership and validation

## Days 31–60 — Build market and delivery readiness

### Sales

- Build warm-network account and referral list
- Prepare assessment, Foundation Core, and Full Foundation one-pagers
- Prepare discovery and qualification guides
- Prepare proposal and statement-of-work packages
- Prepare pricing and complexity-adjustment worksheet
- Begin buyer interviews and outreach
- Seed the Top 200 catalog with at least 20 high-confidence opportunities

### Delivery

- Finalize Foundation implementation checklist using the recovered work breakdown
- Build architecture and security baseline
- Build customer access and offboarding procedures
- Build testing, evidence, acceptance, and handoff templates
- Establish incident and escalation process
- Establish effort-capture and lessons-learned process
- Define the supportability gate for recurring service

### Microsoft, distributor, and partners

- Research current Microsoft partner requirements and recommended sequence
- Complete Pax8 commercial, legal, security, support, billing, integration, and transition diligence
- Test a sample provisioning and reconciliation workflow where possible
- Record the Pax8 approval or rejection decision
- Qualify legal, tax, insurance, audit, networking, and specialist partners
- Define partner governance and commercial terms

## Days 61–90 — Sell and deliver the first pilot

- Convert qualified discovery into a paid assessment or Foundation pilot
- Contract and collect initial payment according to approved terms
- Deliver using the standard solution and delivery architecture
- Measure planned versus actual effort by work area
- Capture customer delay, scope change, rework, warranty, and partner cost
- Capture configuration, evidence, risks, exceptions, outcomes, and acceptance
- Produce a case study or anonymized proof point where permitted
- Propose recurring Microsoft Security and Governance
- Update service scope, prerequisites, pricing, and delivery standards from evidence
- Add lessons and reusable solution patterns to the repository

## 90-day success criteria

- Current owners and participants identified
- Role functions and interim decision rights assigned
- Legal entity established
- Banking, accounting, insurance, and core contracts operational
- Assessment, Foundation Core, and Full Foundation approved and documented
- Cost, capacity, price, and margin models reviewed
- Internal security baseline operational
- Pax8 diligence completed with an approved decision or documented rejection and alternative
- Microsoft partner sequence documented
- Critical independent and specialist partners qualified
- Active qualified pipeline
- At least one paid pilot contracted or delivered
- Planned-versus-actual effort captured
- Lessons and reusable assets incorporated into the repository
