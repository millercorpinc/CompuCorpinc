# 90-Day Launch Plan

## Objective

Establish [COMPANY_NAME] as a legally and operationally viable business, launch the first productized service, and secure the first paid pilot customer.

## Days 1–30 — Formalize and prepare

### Founders and legal

- Finalize company name
- Confirm founder roles and legal participants
- Obtain legal and tax advice
- Form the entity
- Establish founder, IP, and decision documents

### Finance and risk

- Obtain EIN and required registrations
- Open business banking
- Establish accounting and expense controls
- Obtain initial insurance
- Prepare MSA, SOW, NDA, partner, and subcontractor templates

### Commercial

- Approve the initial target customer
- Approve the initial entry offer
- Finalize service scope, exclusions, prerequisites, and acceptance
- Build cost, capacity, and pricing models

### Technology

- Establish secure company identity and endpoints
- Establish collaboration and document management
- Establish secret management
- Select minimal CRM, project/service management, documentation, and finance capabilities

## Days 31–60 — Build market and delivery readiness

### Sales

- Build warm-network account list
- Prepare one-page offer
- Prepare discovery questionnaire
- Prepare proposal and statement-of-work package
- Begin buyer interviews and outreach

### Delivery

- Build implementation checklist
- Build architecture and security baseline
- Build customer access and offboarding procedures
- Build testing, acceptance, and handoff templates
- Establish incident and escalation process

### Partners

- Research Microsoft partner requirements
- Evaluate indirect CSP/distributor candidates
- Qualify legal, tax, insurance, audit, networking, and specialist partners
- Define partner governance and commercial terms

## Days 61–90 — Sell and deliver the first pilot

- Convert qualified discovery into a paid pilot
- Contract and collect initial payment according to approved terms
- Deliver using the standard process
- Measure planned versus actual effort
- Capture risks, outcomes, and customer feedback
- Produce a case study or anonymized proof point where permitted
- Propose recurring governance
- Update service scope, pricing, and delivery standards from evidence

## 90-day success criteria

- Legal entity established
- Banking, accounting, insurance, and core contracts operational
- Initial service approved and documented
- Internal security baseline operational
- Distributor and critical partner pathway identified
- Active qualified pipeline
- At least one paid pilot contracted or delivered
- Lessons captured in the repository
