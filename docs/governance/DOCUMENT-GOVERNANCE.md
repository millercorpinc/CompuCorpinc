# Document Governance

## Document classes

- **Canonical:** authoritative current direction
- **Decision record:** why a material choice was made
- **Working draft:** under active development
- **Template:** reusable structure
- **Historical:** context not currently governing
- **External reference:** third-party material

## Required metadata

Canonical documents should state:

- Purpose
- Owner
- Status
- Source
- Last reviewed
- Next review
- Related decisions
- Open questions

The initial files omit formal owner fields until founder roles are approved. Add them after governance is settled.

## Review cadence

- Strategy and services: quarterly
- Security and risk: quarterly or after material change
- Partner profiles: at least annually
- Pricing and cost models: quarterly during launch
- Templates: after each use
- Decision records: immutable except for status and superseding links
