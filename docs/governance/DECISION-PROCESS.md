# Decision Process

## When to create a decision record

Create one when a choice materially affects:

- Ownership or governance
- Market position
- Service scope
- Pricing
- Legal or commercial risk
- Partner commitments
- Technology architecture
- Security or data handling
- Operating cost
- Customer experience
- Reversibility or migration effort

## Process

1. State the decision and owner.
2. Describe context and constraints.
3. List options.
4. Evaluate benefits, costs, risks, and reversibility.
5. Identify required external advice or evidence.
6. Approve, reject, defer, or time-box an experiment.
7. Record consequences.
8. Update canonical documents.
9. Link implementation work.
10. Define review or expiration if appropriate.
