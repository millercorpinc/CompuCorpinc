# Legal, Finance, and Risk Setup

This is an execution checklist, not legal, tax, accounting, or insurance advice.

## Legal and entity

- Name availability and trademark screening
- Entity and jurisdiction analysis
- Formation
- Registered agent
- Operating or shareholder agreement
- Foreign qualifications where required
- Local licenses and registrations
- Intellectual-property assignments
- Contractor and employee documents

## Tax and finance

- EIN and tax registrations
- Tax election analysis
- Business bank account
- Accounting system and chart of accounts
- Expense and reimbursement policy
- Invoicing and collections
- Payroll when required
- Sales-tax and license-resale analysis
- Financial forecast
- Capital and cash-reserve plan

## Insurance

Discuss with a qualified broker:

- General liability
- Professional liability / E&O
- Cyber liability
- Workers compensation
- Crime/fidelity
- Employment practices
- Directors and officers where appropriate
- Auto or umbrella where relevant

## Contract framework

- Master services agreement
- Statements of work
- Managed-services schedule
- Service-level schedule
- Data protection and security terms
- Confidentiality agreement
- Subcontractor agreement
- Partner/referral agreement
- Acceptable-use and access authorization
- Change-order form
- Customer offboarding and transition terms
