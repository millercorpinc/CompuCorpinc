# Founder Governance Setup

## Actions

1. Identify all proposed founders and their expected contributions.
2. Define full-time, part-time, employee, contractor, and investor expectations.
3. Agree on functional accountability.
4. Model equity and vesting alternatives.
5. Define capital contributions and expense treatment.
6. Define compensation and distribution principles.
7. Define voting and reserved decisions.
8. Define contracting and spending authority.
9. Define intellectual-property ownership.
10. Define confidentiality, solicitation, and customer ownership.
11. Define departure, disability, death, deadlock, and buyout mechanisms.
12. Take the agreed business terms to qualified counsel.
13. Record the approved structure in a formal decision record.

## Deliverables

- Founder term sheet
- Role-accountability matrix
- Decision-rights matrix
- Capital and compensation model
- Counsel-reviewed founder agreement
