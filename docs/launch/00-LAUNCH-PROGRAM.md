# Launch Program

## Objective

Move from developed concept to a legally established, commercially ready, secure, and deliverable business.

## Workstreams

1. Founder governance
2. Name, brand, and intellectual property
3. Legal entity and tax
4. Finance and insurance
5. Contracts and risk
6. Service and pricing
7. Partner and distributor ecosystem
8. Internal technology and security
9. Sales and marketing
10. Delivery operations
11. Pilot clients and evidence
12. Hiring and specialist capacity

## Stage gates

### Gate 1 — Founder alignment

Required:

- Founder list and roles
- Decision rights
- Ownership principles
- Conflict and departure process
- Initial capital and compensation principles

### Gate 2 — Legal readiness

Required:

- Approved name
- Entity formed
- Tax and banking setup
- Core insurance
- Contract framework under review or approved

### Gate 3 — Commercial readiness

Required:

- Initial customer and offer
- Scope and exclusions
- Pricing model
- Discovery and proposal process
- Sales materials
- Partner dependencies understood

### Gate 4 — Delivery readiness

Required:

- Internal security baseline
- Delivery checklist
- Documentation templates
- Customer access process
- Testing and acceptance
- Escalation and incident process
- Capacity confirmed

### Gate 5 — Pilot launch

Required:

- Qualified opportunity
- Signed agreement
- Delivery owner
- Success measures
- Evidence capture plan
- Post-engagement review
