# Initial Service Launch

## Decision required

Choose one primary entry offer and one secondary expansion offer.

## Recommended candidates for validation

### Option A — Assessment first

Paid technology, security, and governance assessment leading to a roadmap and remediation proposal.

Advantages:

- Lower trust barrier
- Better scoping
- Reveals expansion work
- Useful across varied environments

Risks:

- Can become generic consulting
- Customer may not fund implementation
- Requires a strong differentiated deliverable

### Option B — Secure workplace foundation

Defined implementation that establishes a governed cloud-workplace baseline.

This is the recommended primary launch offer. It should be packaged in three tiers based on customer size and complexity:

- Foundation Core — 1–9 users, roughly 26–32 execution hours, approximately $6,000–$9,000
- Foundation (Full) — 10–25 users, roughly 46 execution hours, approximately $12,000–$18,000
- Foundation (Complex) — 25+ users or higher-risk environments, 60+ execution hours or more, approximately $18,000+

Advantages:

- Tangible outcome
- Reusable delivery
- Natural recurring attach
- Demonstrates technical depth
- Clear difference between standard, small-company, and higher-risk implementations

Risks:

- Existing environments vary
- Takeover risks can destroy fixed-fee margin
- Requires firm prerequisites and exclusions
- Requires a defined customer and acceptance model

### Option C — Fractional technology roadmap

Executive advisory engagement with continuing governance.

Advantages:

- Strong strategic position
- Recurring potential
- Low infrastructure requirement

Risks:

- Harder to sell without reputation
- Scope can expand ambiguously
- Requires executive access and trust

## Launch deliverables

- Service brief
- Discovery questionnaire
- Qualification rules
- Scope and exclusions
- Assumptions
- Delivery checklist
- Architecture pattern
- Estimate model
- Proposal language
- Acceptance criteria
- Recurring attach
- Pilot evidence plan
