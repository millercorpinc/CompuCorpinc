# Founder Term Sheet (Draft)

## Source and status

- Status: draft working recommendation
- Purpose: define the initial founder deal structure in a short, reviewable form
- Scope: for founding contributors and early team roles only
- Not legal advice: requires counsel review before execution

## Summary

This term sheet documents the working founder structure for the company. It is designed to preserve founder control while recognizing that equity should be earned based on actual value, not simply because people are friends or enthusiastic at the start.

## Working assumptions

- The founder taking legal and financial responsibility retains final control.
- No founder receives equal equity by default.
- Sales and execution are distinct roles with different compensation and ownership logic.
- Equity is earned and vested based on real company outcomes.
- The company will reserve a future-use pool for hires or strategic contributors after the earliest team is established.

## Recommended private structure

### Founder control

- Founder-controlled voting rights: 51% minimum
- Founder retains final authority over contracts, hiring, financial decisions, equity issuance, and business direction
- No early contributor can bind the company without written authorization

### Sales and execution pools

- Sales pool: 20%
- Execution pool: 20%
- Reserved future-use buffer: 9% (not an advisor pool)
- Dedicated advisor pool: none
- Founder-controlled equity: 51%

### Role expectations

#### Founder / controlling owner

- carries legal and operational risk
- retains final authority over company direction
- sets strategy, risk posture, prices, and compensation policy
- may exercise final decision rights on equity allocation and deal attribution

#### Founding sales lead

- responsible for sales strategy and revenue generation
- earns based on paid client work or approved revenue-linked incentives
- should be compensated primarily through commission and a capped sales equity allocation

#### Founding execution contributors

- responsible for delivery, implementation, and operation of client work
- earn based on actual project profit and work value creation
- should be compensated by project value and a capped execution equity allocation

## Incentive logic

- Equity is a performance tool, not a default founder gift.
- Sales contributors are rewarded for revenue generation.
- Execution contributors are rewarded for profit creation and delivery quality.
- Equity does not vest for interest, future promises, or pipeline value alone.

## Guardrails

- equity vests only after a Qualifying Deal is paid and accepted;
- each contributor has a role-specific cap;
- unvested equity remains with the company;
- contributor titles do not equal final ownership rights;
- role definitions can be updated, but the founder remains the final authority.

## Next legal actions

The following items should be reviewed by qualified counsel before final agreement:

1. entity type and tax structure;
2. founder equity issuance timing;
3. voting and reserved decisions;
4. founder departure and buyout terms;
5. IP assignment and confidentiality provisions;
6. independent contractor or employee treatment;
7. material contract authority and spending thresholds;
8. repurchase and clawback rights for unvested equity.

## Decision record

This term sheet is a working draft. It should be reviewed with counsel and converted into a formal founder agreement before any founder equity is issued or any contributor is treated as a co-owner.
