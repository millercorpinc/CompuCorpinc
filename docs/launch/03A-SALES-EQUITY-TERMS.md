# Sales Equity Terms (Draft Example)

## Source and status

- Status: draft working recommendation for founder and counsel review
- Source: January 2026 Startup discussions plus August 18, 2026 founder-equity refinement thread
- Purpose: define the companion deal-based model for the initial founding sales lead
- Scope: the first founding cohort only; not a standing equity plan for every future salesperson
- Not legal advice: use only as a working template for qualified legal and tax review

## Working commercial design

The initial team is expected to include one strong founding sales lead and multiple execution contributors. Sales and execution use separate company-wide pools because they create different kinds of value.

- Total Sales Equity Pool: 20% maximum of fully diluted company equity
- Strong founding sales-lead cap: 15% working maximum
- Early sales-contributor range: 10–12%
- Standard sales-contributor range: 4–6%
- Dedicated advisor pool: none
- Founder control floor: 51% of voting power

The pool is not an individual grant. No contributor automatically receives the unused portion of the pool.

## Draft language

This Sales Equity Vesting Agreement (this Agreement) is entered into by and between:

- Company: [COMPANY_NAME]
- Contributor: [CONTRIBUTOR NAME]
- Effective Date: [DATE]

### 1. Relationship and authority

Contributor is engaged to perform sales, relationship, discovery, and commercial-ownership services. The agreement does not by itself create employment, partnership, authority to bind the Company, or voting control. Any employment or contractor relationship must be documented separately.

### 2. Founder control

The Founder shall retain at least fifty-one percent (51%) of the Company’s voting power unless the Founder approves a different arrangement in writing. Contributor may not sign contracts, change prices, issue equity, commit delivery resources, or bind the Company without written authority.

### 3. Sales equity pool

Contributor may earn equity only from the Sales Equity Pool. The aggregate Sales Equity Pool shall not exceed twenty percent (20%) of the Company’s fully diluted equity.

Contributor’s individual cap is [INDIVIDUAL CAP]%. The cap is a maximum, not a guaranteed grant.

### 4. Qualifying Sales Deal

A Qualifying Sales Deal must satisfy all of the following:

- a client agreement approved by the Company is executed;
- required customer payment is received and cleared;
- the deal is not cancelled, refunded, rescinded, or written off;
- Contributor’s commercial contribution is documented and approved;
- the deal meets pricing, risk, scope, and acceptance rules established by the Company.

Pipeline, introductions alone, unpaid proposals, verbal commitments, future renewals, and uncollected revenue do not qualify.

### 5. Sales attribution

Sales credit may reflect:

- origination of the relationship;
- qualification and discovery;
- commercial strategy;
- proposal leadership;
- negotiation and close;
- account ownership through payment and handoff.

The Founder or founder-controlled authority determines attribution when more than one person contributed. A referral fee, commission, or equity award may be divided to prevent double-crediting the same value.

### 6. Deal-based vesting

Equity vests only for Qualifying Sales Deals. The exact per-deal rate or formula must be approved in a written deal schedule before the deal is counted.

The deal schedule must state:

- eligible revenue or gross-profit basis;
- Contributor attribution percentage;
- cash commission, if any;
- equity allocation or formula;
- vesting and forfeiture conditions;
- individual and pool cap remaining after the deal.

No default per-deal percentage is promised by this agreement.

### 7. Commission and equity are separate

Cash commission and equity compensate different risks and time horizons. A deal may earn commission, equity, both, or neither according to an approved written schedule.

The Company shall avoid automatically awarding full cash commission and full equity credit for the same contribution without reviewing the combined economics. Any commission plan must define payment timing, refund or clawback treatment, and interaction with equity.

### 8. Renewals, expansions, and recurring revenue

Renewal, expansion, or recurring-service equity does not vest automatically. A written schedule must define whether the Contributor retains account ownership and what ongoing commercial responsibility is required.

Passive revenue, licensing renewals, or recurring charges do not create perpetual equity rights.

### 9. Individual and pool caps

- No award may cause Contributor to exceed the individual cap.
- No award may cause aggregate sales awards to exceed the 20% Sales Equity Pool.
- Unused pool equity remains unissued and does not transfer automatically to Contributor.
- Reaching a cap ends additional equity vesting unless the Founder approves a written exception.

### 10. No retroactive vesting

No equity vests for work performed before the Effective Date, historical introductions, prior conversations, informal commitments, or deals without an approved written schedule.

### 11. Delivery protection

Sales credit does not permit Contributor to promise unapproved scope, discounts, service levels, delivery dates, security exceptions, or legal terms. Material misrepresentation or unauthorized commitments may disqualify a deal and trigger any lawful clawback or disciplinary provisions in the final agreement.

### 12. Termination

Upon termination:

- Contributor retains only vested equity, subject to the final governing documents;
- unvested equity and unused capacity under the cap remain with the Company;
- no future pipeline, renewal, or account revenue vests after termination unless a written agreement expressly provides otherwise;
- Contributor loses all authority to represent or bind the Company.

### 13. Confidentiality, IP, and customer ownership

Contributor must protect confidential information, assign company work product as required by the final agreement, use approved systems, and follow documented customer-ownership and non-solicitation rules that have been reviewed for enforceability.

### 14. Governing documents and professional review

The final arrangement must be coordinated with the Company’s entity documents, capitalization table, tax treatment, securities-law obligations, employment or contractor agreement, commission plan, and founder agreement.

If this draft conflicts with executed governing documents, the executed documents control.

## Required decisions before execution

- identity of the founding sales lead;
- individual cap within the working role ranges;
- revenue versus gross-profit basis;
- per-deal vesting formula and expected deal-count horizon;
- commission rate and combined compensation ceiling;
- treatment of multi-contributor deals;
- renewals, expansions, refunds, and clawbacks;
- voting versus non-voting instrument;
- tax and securities treatment;
- departure and repurchase terms.

This draft records the working business design without pretending that unapproved legal or economic terms are settled.
