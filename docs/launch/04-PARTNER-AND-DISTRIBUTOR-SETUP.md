# Partner and Distributor Setup

## Distributor evaluation

Compare:

- Supported products and regions
- Microsoft licensing capability
- Billing and taxation support
- Provisioning automation
- Support and escalation quality
- Partner enablement
- Contract terms
- Credit and payment requirements
- Security and data handling
- API and integration capability
- Migration and termination process
- Economics and incentives

Do not select solely on margin.

## Partner setup sequence

1. List capabilities that will not be internal at launch.
2. Identify which require independence or credentials.
3. Define partner qualification criteria.
4. Identify two or more candidates for critical categories.
5. Review capabilities, references, security, insurance, and contracts.
6. Define delivery, escalation, customer ownership, and economics.
7. Pilot the relationship.
8. Review performance and concentration risk.

## Initial categories

- Legal
- Tax/accounting
- Insurance
- Compliance/audit
- Cybersecurity specialist
- Networking/field services
- Development/data specialist
- Telecom
- Hardware procurement
- Distributor/CSP
