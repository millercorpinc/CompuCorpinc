# Sales and Pilot Program

## Current proposed launch motion

Start in Chicago and Chicagoland with four parallel prospect tracks:

1. Physicians and clinics
2. CPA and accounting firms
3. Construction and field services
4. Law firms and other regulated professional firms

Use approximately five accounts per track as a flexible starting point. Treat this as a market test, not a permanent vertical decision. Compare response, urgency, buying access, delivery complexity, price acceptance, and referral potential. The detailed offer and module boundaries are in `docs/launch/07-CHICAGO-MARKET-AND-MICROSOFT-ASSESSMENT-BRIEF.md`.

Lead with a paid, fixed-fee Technology and Security Baseline Assessment. The core Microsoft baseline is designed as one week; business-systems, vertical-risk, and migration/takeover discovery are separately scoped modules. Require assessment evidence before fixing migration or takeover pricing.

## Warm-market launch

- Build a list of trusted professional contacts.
- Classify likely buyer, introducer, partner, or advisor.
- Prepare a concise problem/outcome message.
- Ask for discovery conversations, not generic referrals.
- Record objections and language.
- Offer a paid pilot when fit is clear.

## Pilot selection criteria

- Real business urgency
- Decision-maker access
- Environment within delivery capability
- Manageable scope
- Willingness to provide evidence and feedback
- Reasonable payment terms
- No unacceptable security or legal risk
- Potential for recurring work or case-study value

## Pilot review

Capture:

- Planned versus actual effort
- Scope changes
- Risks and incidents
- Customer participation
- Outcome evidence
- Margin
- Reusable assets
- Documentation gaps
- Pricing lessons
- Recurring opportunity
- Prospect track and module selected
- Assessment-to-implementation conversion
- Whether the customer permits an anonymized proof point or case study
