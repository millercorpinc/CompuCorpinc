# Legal Implementation Gates

## Gate A — no equity promises yet

Do not promise or issue founder/contributor equity until:

- true founders are identified;
- entity/tax architecture is professionally reviewed;
- Operating Agreement governance is designed;
- incentive instrument is selected;
- securities/tax workflow is established.

## Gate B — no worker starts without status review

Before an early worker performs recurring company work:

- classify employee/contractor/tax-partner status;
- execute the correct agreement;
- complete payroll/registration or freelancer requirements;
- complete IP/confidentiality/security onboarding;
- resolve Michigan requirements when applicable.

## Gate C — no paid client without contract/insurance baseline

Before first paid delivery:

- approved MSA/SOW/change-order stack;
- administrative-access/security terms;
- liability/indemnity aligned with insurance;
- customer-data classification and regulated-client triggers checked;
- Chicago operating license/location resolved.

## Gate D — no cloud resale without tax architecture

Before material cloud/software resale:

- distributor/reseller legal role confirmed;
- Chicago PPTLT reviewed;
- Illinois lease/software treatment reviewed;
- sourcing/collection/remittance party documented;
- contract and invoice design approved.

## Gate E — no regulated-data access without module

Before accessing:

- ePHI: determine BA status and execute BAA where required;
- GLBA customer information: execute required service-provider security terms;
- biometric data: BIPA review;
- privileged law-firm/client data: customer confidentiality/security requirements documented.

## Gate F — no decision marked approved without record

After counsel/CPA review:

- update ADR-0005 or create superseding decision;
- update Decision Status;
- close resolved Open Questions;
- update launch backlog;
- retain professional closing memo/evidence securely outside public GitHub.
