## Customer-contract law and general commercial law

These regimes are not necessarily unique to this company but should be reviewed when counsel drafts the MSA/SOW stack:

- Illinois contract/common law;
- Illinois Uniform Commercial Code where transactions include goods/software rights that bring UCC provisions into scope;
- electronic signature law / E-SIGN and Illinois Electronic Commerce Security Act as applicable;
- limitation-of-liability and indemnity enforceability;
- interest/late-fee rules;
- consumer-protection law if the company ever sells to individuals rather than businesses;
- state data-breach laws based on affected residents, not merely company formation state.

## Corporate housekeeping evidence

Retain permanently in a secure corporate record system:

- formation certificate/articles;
- Operating Agreement and amendments;
- member/manager consents;
- unit ledger and grant approvals;
- tax elections;
- securities exemption/notice records;
- annual reports/good-standing certificates;
- insurance policies;
- major contracts;
- IP assignments;
- material licenses;
- tax registrations;
- foreign qualifications;
- business licenses;
- cap-table backups.

Do not rely on GitHub as the sole repository for signed confidential legal documents or personal tax information. The repository should track the document architecture, status, and non-sensitive evidence references; executed sensitive documents should live in an access-controlled corporate record system.

## Revalidation cadence

- **At formation:** re-check every P0 source.
- **Before first employee/contractor:** re-check worker/restrictive covenant sources.
- **Before first Chicago cloud resale:** re-check Chicago and Illinois tax sources.
- **Before first healthcare/financial regulated client:** re-check sector rules and customer-specific requirements.
- **Before any new equity grant:** re-check securities/tax plan assumptions.
- **Annually:** review formation-state, foreign qualification, licenses, tax rules, privacy/security law, and standard contracts.
