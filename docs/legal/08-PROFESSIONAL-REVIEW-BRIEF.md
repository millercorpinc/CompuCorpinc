# Professional Review Brief

## Purpose

Give Illinois startup/business counsel and the company's CPA/tax advisor a concise instruction set for reviewing the legal architecture without requiring them to reconstruct the business model from scratch.

## Business facts assumed by the research

- Founder-led technology advisory, implementation, security, automation, and managed-governance company.
- Chicago/Chicagoland is the proposed initial market and likely operating center.
- Some proposed founders/contributors are in Michigan.
- No current institutional venture-capital requirement has been identified.
- Microsoft/cloud distribution or indirect-CSP resale may become part of the commercial model.
- Early staffing may combine founders, employees, contractors/specialists, and performance incentives.
- Existing business planning used a 51% controlling-founder / 20% sales / 20% execution / 9% reserve model, but those percentages remain unapproved planning economics.
- `CompuCorp` is only a temporary codename; final legal/public name is open.

## Proposed architecture to test

Please confirm, modify, or reject each item rather than returning a generic entity recommendation:

1. Illinois manager-managed LLC as the launch entity.
2. Federal partnership tax classification initially.
3. Founder control implemented through manager appointment/removal, voting classes if appropriate, and reserved matters rather than relying on 51% alone.
4. True founder ownership separated from contributor incentive compensation.
5. Founder units subject to negotiated vesting/company repurchase rights for unearned ownership.
6. Profits-interest/incentive-unit plan versus phantom equity for long-term non-founder contributors.
7. Cash commission/profit sharing as default for early outside sales/intermittent contributors unless actual equity is deliberately approved.
8. Michigan foreign qualification/payroll/tax handled by an activity-trigger matrix.
9. Delaware deferred unless financing/M&A facts create a concrete reason.
10. S-corporation election reconsidered only after the economic/incentive structure stabilizes.

## Questions for business counsel

Please provide a written answer to:

- Which Illinois LLC Act rules relevant to manager appointment/removal, member voting, admission, redemption, amendments, information rights, fiduciary duties/conflicts, and minority remedies can and cannot be modified by the Operating Agreement?
- What governance mechanism best preserves intended founder control through future incentive grants and dilution?
- Which people should legally be members at formation?
- What founder vesting/repurchase instrument is appropriate for each true founder?
- What departure/death/disability/divorce/bankruptcy/transfer/buyout mechanics should be used?
- Should vested departing members remain members, become transferees only, or be subject to a company/member call right?
- Which incentive instrument best reproduces the desired deal-earned economics without creating unnecessary member/K-1 complexity?
- For each actual equity/incentive category, what federal and Illinois/Michigan securities exemption/notice process applies?
- What worker-classification process should precede engagement of recurring execution contributors?
- Which Illinois restrictive-covenant/IP/statutory notices belong in employee and contractor forms?
- What Chicago license/home-office determination should be documented based on the final business address?
- What foreign-qualification triggers should be used for Michigan?
- Which customer contract provisions need special treatment because the company will have privileged administrative access?
- Which HIPAA/BAA, GLBA/Safeguards, law-firm confidentiality, BIPA, or other regulated-client modules should be prepared at launch versus only when triggered?

## Questions for CPA/tax advisor

Please provide a side-by-side model for:

- partnership-taxed Illinois LLC;
- same LLC with a later S election, if feasible;
- C-corporation alternative only if there is a credible strategic reason.

Include:

- founder/member compensation;
- self-employment/payroll taxes;
- guaranteed payments;
- tax distributions;
- profits-interest tax treatment and grant administration;
- capital-account requirements;
- Illinois 1.5% replacement tax;
- Illinois PTE election under current 2026 law;
- Illinois pass-through withholding for Michigan/nonresident owners;
- Michigan owner taxation/credits;
- expected annual compliance cost;
- impact of adding incentive holders;
- reasonable-compensation requirements if an S election is later made;
- effect of S-corporation one-class-of-stock requirements on proposed incentives;
- Chicago/local tax issues.

## Cloud/software tax memo requested

Before material reseller/CSP activity, return a written analysis of:

- whether the company is reseller/lessor, agent, billing intermediary, or referral partner;
- Chicago Personal Property Lease Transaction Tax applicability and sourcing;
- current 15% Chicago rate where a taxable lease applies;
- nonpossessory computer lease classification;
- mandatory versus optional support/governance charges;
- re-lease/resale documentation;
- small-new-business exemption, if available and worthwhile;
- Illinois state lease/software treatment;
- registration/collection/remittance responsibility;
- invoice structure and tax line items;
- contract language for tax changes and customer exemptions.

## Deliverables requested from professionals

The preferred output is not just advice. Return:

1. short written entity/tax recommendation;
2. redline/response to ADR-0005;
3. founder governance/economic term sheet;
4. draft Operating Agreement;
5. founder ownership/vesting/IP package;
6. selected incentive plan and award form, or phantom/cash plan;
7. equity/securities issuance checklist;
8. Illinois/Michigan trigger matrix;
9. worker classification/onboarding checklist;
10. modular customer contract package;
11. insurance/liability coordination notes;
12. Chicago license and cloud-tax determinations;
13. formation closing checklist.
