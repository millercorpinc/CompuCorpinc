# Do Not Sign Repository Legal Drafts

The legal/equity language in this repository is for research, requirements, issue spotting, economic design, and counsel review.

It is **not approved for signature or issuance**.

Do not use repository drafts to:

- issue membership units, profits interests, options, or other securities;
- promise a contributor a legally binding ownership percentage;
- classify a worker as an independent contractor;
- bind a founder to final vesting/buyout terms;
- sign a customer MSA/BAA/DPA;
- make a tax election;
- file a securities exemption/notice;
- conclude that Michigan qualification is or is not required;
- conclude that a cloud/software charge is or is not taxable.

The repository should be used to give qualified professionals a precise specification and to record approved decisions after review.
