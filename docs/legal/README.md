## Current proposed architecture — not approved

Subject to founder approval and professional validation:

- Illinois manager-managed LLC;
- initial federal partnership tax classification;
- control implemented through the Operating Agreement and manager/reserved-matter mechanics, not percentage alone;
- true founder ownership separated from contributor incentive compensation;
- actual founder units for true founders;
- profits interests/incentive units versus phantom equity evaluated for long-term non-founder contributors;
- cash commission/profit sharing favored for shorter-term sales/intermittent contributors unless equity is deliberately approved;
- Delaware deferred absent a concrete financing/investor reason;
- Michigan treated as an activity-based qualification/payroll/tax trigger;
- modular customer contract stack rather than one generic agreement.

## Do not sign repository drafts

The documents in this repository are business requirements, research, example clauses, and decision support. They are not approved forms for signature.

Before execution, qualified counsel/CPA should return the actual coordinated package, including as applicable:

- Operating Agreement;
- founder purchase/restricted-unit documents;
- IP/confidentiality/invention assignment documents;
- incentive plan and award agreement or phantom plan;
- securities exemption/approval package;
- worker agreements;
- MSA/SOW/change-order/security/access/reseller schedules;
- regulated-client modules;
- formation/tax/registration closing set.

Executed confidential documents, signatures, personal information, tax forms, cap-table details, and customer-sensitive contracts should live in an access-controlled corporate record system rather than this public repository.
