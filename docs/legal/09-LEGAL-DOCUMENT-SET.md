# Governing Legal Document Set

## Purpose

Map the legal documents that should govern the company after the founders approve the business terms. This is a requirements map, not a set of signable forms.

## Corporate / LLC record hierarchy

For an Illinois LLC, counsel should coordinate at least:

1. **Articles of Organization** — state formation record.
2. **Operating Agreement** — primary private governance/economic agreement among company and members.
3. **Initial member/manager written consents** — formation, bank, tax, officers/authority, unit issuance, plan adoption, material contracts.
4. **Unit ledger / capitalization record** — authoritative ownership and grant record.
5. **Founder Unit Purchase / Subscription Agreements** — each founder's acquisition of units, consideration, representations, and tax acknowledgements.
6. **Restricted-unit / repurchase provisions** — vesting and company repurchase of unearned founder ownership, either in purchase agreement or coordinated document.
7. **Founder IP / Confidentiality / Invention Assignment** — company ownership of relevant work product and protection of confidential information, subject to statutory limits.
8. **Incentive plan** — profits-interest/incentive-unit plan or phantom-equity plan if adopted.
9. **Award Agreements** — participant-specific awards under the approved plan.
10. **Operating Agreement joinders** — when an award results in actual member status or other OA-bound rights.
11. **Securities exemption / approval records** — grant-by-grant compliance closing set.
12. **Tax elections and acknowledgements** — including any Section 83(b) process where applicable.
13. **Buy-sell / transfer provisions** — integrated in the OA or companion agreement.
14. **Annual/transaction consents and minutes** — permanent governance record.

## What the Operating Agreement should govern

At minimum:

- manager-managed status;
- manager identity and powers;
- appointment/removal/replacement of managers;
- member classes and voting rights;
- ordinary-course authority;
- reserved matters;
- capital contributions;
- units/percentage interests and denominator definitions;
- allocations;
- distributions and tax distributions;
- admission of new members;
- issuance authority;
- incentive-plan class rights if actual LLC interests are used;
- books, records, information rights, and confidentiality;
- fiduciary/conflict/related-party process;
- indemnification/exculpation to the extent lawful;
- tax representative/partnership representative;
- transfers, ROFR, prohibited transfers;
- dissociation/departure;
- death/disability;
- divorce/charging-order response;
- bankruptcy;
- company/member call rights;
- valuation/buyout process;
- deadlock if applicable;
- mergers/conversions/sale/dissolution;
- amendments;
- dispute resolution;
- notices;
- governing law.

## What should not be left only in a contributor contract

The following company-level rights need to match the Operating Agreement and formal approvals:

- who has authority to issue equity;
- whether an award creates member status;
- voting/non-voting rights;
- distribution/liquidation rights;
- transfer restrictions;
- company repurchase/call rights;
- manager appointment/removal;
- admission of new members;
- tax allocations/distributions;
- amendment rights;
- treatment on company sale/dissolution.

A contributor agreement cannot safely create an economic promise that contradicts the Operating Agreement or an actual unit class.

## Worker / contributor documents

Depending on actual status:

### Employee

- offer/employment agreement as appropriate;
- confidentiality/IP/invention assignment with Illinois-required notices;
- handbook/policies as company grows;
- commission/incentive plan if applicable;
- equity/phantom Award Agreement if approved;
- restrictive covenant only if role, compensation, current law, consideration, and procedure support it;
- security/acceptable-use/administrative-access policy acknowledgements.

### Independent contractor / subcontractor

- contractor master agreement;
- project SOW;
- fee/payment terms satisfying applicable freelancer law;
- classification facts supporting contractor treatment;
- no authority to bind company;
- confidentiality/IP/background-IP schedule;
- security/access obligations;
- customer flow-down terms;
- incident reporting;
- BAA/DPA flow-down when triggered;
- insurance where justified;
- approved incentive Award Agreement only if separately authorized.

### Tax partner / profits-interest holder

- plan;
- Award Agreement;
- OA joinder/class provisions;
- tax acknowledgement;
- K-1/tax-distribution treatment;
- vesting/forfeiture/call mechanics;
- securities closing record;
- worker-service agreement only insofar as consistent with partner tax/legal status.

## Customer contract hierarchy

Recommended order of precedence should be deliberately stated, for example:

1. signed change order or amendment that expressly overrides identified terms;
2. applicable SOW;
3. regulated-data/service schedule for the specific engagement;
4. MSA;
5. incorporated third-party/vendor terms where applicable.

Counsel may choose another hierarchy, but it should be explicit.

### Customer package

- MSA;
- SOW;
- change order;
- administrative-access authorization;
- security/data protection addendum;
- managed-services/governance schedule;
- SLA/service-level schedule when sold;
- reseller/cloud schedule;
- HIPAA BAA when triggered;
- other regulated-client addendum when required;
- subcontractor/customer flow-down schedule when necessary;
- transition/offboarding terms.

## Commercial and governance policies that support the contracts

- signature/contract-authority matrix;
- spending/debt authority matrix;
- pricing/discount authority;
- commission and attribution policy;
- incentive-credit policy;
- change-order policy;
- tax/exemption-certificate procedure;
- invoice/collections/suspension policy;
- customer access policy;
- data-classification/handling policy;
- incident response;
- vendor/subcontractor risk process;
- records-retention/legal-hold process;
- insurance certificate/renewal process;
- offboarding/credential revocation process.

## Evidence of a legally ready launch

Gate 2 should not mean merely `the LLC exists`.

Legal readiness evidence should include:

- entity active and in good standing;
- Operating Agreement executed;
- founder ownership/IP documents executed;
- cap/unit ledger reconciled;
- tax classification/registrations completed;
- current BOI determination documented;
- required Chicago/location licensing handled;
- Michigan trigger determination documented if applicable;
- insurance bound;
- bank/signing authority documented;
- customer contract stack approved;
- worker templates approved;
- incentive/securities procedure approved before any grants;
- cloud/software tax architecture approved before reseller activity;
- secure corporate-record location established.
