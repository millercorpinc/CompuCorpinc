The website presents Harvey Miller through two audience-specific views without creating competing factual records.

1. `site/harvey-resume.html` is a conventional career and hiring view. It emphasizes role progression, scope, quantified outcomes, capabilities, technology, and education.
2. `site/harvey-founder.html` is a client-facing founder profile. It emphasizes judgment, operating philosophy, client outcomes, delivery posture, and the connection between advisory, implementation, and recurring governance.

Both views share the existing site design system and link directly to each other.
