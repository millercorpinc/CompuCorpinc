## Established business baseline

Treat these as presumptive operating design:

- Advisory-led technology company
- Standardized implementation followed by recurring governance
- Microsoft specialization with vendor-flexible integration
- Primary 10–150-employee customer range
- Fractional leadership, secure workplace, virtual workspace, security/compliance readiness, integration/automation, and managed governance services
- Project-to-recurring revenue architecture
- Sales → Strategy → Operations delivery flow
- Sales Founder / Harvey / Ben functional allocation
- Microsoft, distributor/indirect-CSP, specialist, referral, and independent-professional partner ecosystem
- Founder-led warm-market go-to-market motion
- Repository-first structured knowledge system

## Immediate execution sequence

1. Confirm founder identities, roles, desired economics, and control outcome.
2. Take ADR-0005 and the legal research pack to Illinois startup/business counsel and a CPA/tax advisor; select entity/tax and founder/incentive instruments.
3. Finalize and clear the official company name, then form the entity and execute the coordinated founder/governance/IP package.
4. Establish securities/equity issuance procedure, banking/accounting, registrations, insurance, and the modular contract stack.
5. Resolve Chicago business-license/location requirements and Michigan triggers based on actual operations.
6. Resolve Chicago/Illinois cloud/software tax architecture before material CSP/reseller activity.
7. Approve the assessment/Foundation launch sequence and build the cost, capacity, pricing, and margin model.
8. Establish internal security and minimum operating tools.
9. Select distributor pathway and qualify critical partners.
10. Complete sales/delivery readiness, then acquire and deliver the first paid pilot.
11. Update the service model from actual evidence.

## Next three executable tasks

1. Founders complete the fact sheet and business decisions in `docs/legal/06-COUNSEL-AND-CPA-CLOSING-CHECKLIST.md`.
2. Counsel/CPA review ADR-0005 and return a written side-by-side recommendation for founder units plus profits-interest versus phantom/cash incentives.
3. Before formation or any equity promise, approve the governance/equity architecture enough for counsel to draft the Operating Agreement and founder/incentive documents.
