# ADR-0001: Establish the business category

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-07-15
- **Decision owner:** Founders
- **Approvers:** Founders
- **Related documents:** `docs/business/01-BUSINESS-DEFINITION.md`

## Context

The Startup project consistently developed a company that combines executive technology advice, implementation, security, automation, integration, and recurring governance.

## Options

### A. Conventional managed-services provider

Lead with end-user support and device management.

### B. Project consultancy

Sell one-time architecture and implementation engagements.

### C. Advisory-led technology and managed-governance company

Lead with strategy and foundation work, then attach recurring governance and selective support.

## Proposed decision

Adopt Option C.

## Rationale

It preserves senior strategic differentiation, supports repeatable implementation, creates recurring revenue, and avoids defining the company through commodity support.

## Consequences

- Sales must communicate business outcomes, not merely technical support.
- Services require stronger documentation and architecture.
- Support boundaries must be explicit.
- Founder expertise remains customer-facing.
