# ADR-0002: Microsoft specialization with vendor-flexible integration

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-07-15
- **Decision owner:** Technology / Strategy function
- **Approvers:** Founders
- **Related documents:** `docs/architecture/05-TECHNOLOGY-PRINCIPLES.md`

## Context

Microsoft expertise is a major source of credibility and delivery capability. Customers nevertheless operate many non-Microsoft business systems.

## Options

### A. Microsoft-only

Limit services to Microsoft products.

### B. Generalist, vendor-neutral consultancy

Avoid a primary platform specialization.

### C. Microsoft-specialized, ecosystem-flexible

Use Microsoft as the primary platform while integrating and governing the wider environment.

## Proposed decision

Adopt Option C.

## Rationale

This preserves differentiation and partner leverage while allowing the company to solve complete business problems.

## Consequences

- Non-Microsoft products should generally be described by capability category until selected.
- Integration and partner capabilities become important.
- Delivery standards must account for cross-platform security and support.
