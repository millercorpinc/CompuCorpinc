# ADR-0003: Treat partnerships and distribution as structural capabilities

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-07-15
- **Decision owner:** Founders
- **Approvers:** Founders
- **Related documents:** `docs/business/05-PARTNERSHIPS-AND-DISTRIBUTION.md`

## Context

The company cannot and should not internalize every licensed, credentialed, geographic, and specialist capability.

## Proposed decision

Build formal capabilities for:

- Microsoft partnership
- Cloud distribution / CSP
- Specialist delivery partners
- Independent audit and professional partners
- Referral and channel relationships

Remain neutral until a specific partner is approved.

## Consequences

- Partner governance becomes an operating process.
- Customer accountability and subcontractor roles must be explicit.
- Concentration and independence risks must be reviewed.
- Licensing revenue remains supplemental.
