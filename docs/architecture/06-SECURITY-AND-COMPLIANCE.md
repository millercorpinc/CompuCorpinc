# Security and Compliance Architecture

## Security objectives

- Protect company and customer data
- Separate customer environments
- Restrict privileged access
- Maintain auditability
- Reduce configuration drift
- Support incident response
- Demonstrate reasonable controls
- Meet contractual and regulatory obligations

## Minimum internal domains

- Identity and MFA
- Privileged access
- Device management
- Endpoint protection
- Email and collaboration security
- Data classification and sharing
- Secret management
- Logging and alerting
- Vulnerability and patch management
- Backup and recovery
- Incident response
- Vendor and partner risk
- Secure development and change control
- Business continuity
- Security awareness

## Customer delivery rules

- Document administrative access
- Use least privilege
- Separate named accounts from shared credentials
- Store secrets in approved systems
- Record configuration baselines
- Test changes
- Obtain authorization
- Maintain rollback and recovery considerations
- Record exceptions and residual risk
- Define responsibility after handoff

## Compliance position

The company may provide readiness, technical control design, remediation, evidence support, and continuing maintenance.

The company must not claim to provide formal legal advice, tax advice, or independent audit/attestation unless appropriately qualified and independent.
