# Knowledge and Solution System

## Objective

Convert individual experience into structured, reusable, reviewable company knowledge.

## Initial model

- Use cases
- Solution patterns
- Services
- Consultants / people
- Skills
- Client projects
- Deliverables
- Evidence
- Partners
- Lessons learned

## Required capabilities

- Search by business problem
- Match needs to solution patterns
- Identify required skills and partners
- Reuse scope, assumptions, and architecture
- Generate proposal inputs
- Capture delivery evidence
- Build case studies
- Track revisions and approvals
- Separate confidential customer data from reusable patterns

## Minimal launch implementation

Begin with repository-based Markdown and structured YAML/CSV.

Do not build a complex database until:

- The taxonomy has been used in real engagements
- Search or reporting limitations are clear
- Ownership and maintenance are assigned
- Migration benefits exceed operating cost

## Future platform options

A structured business application or data platform may later manage the objects and relationships. Dataverse was historically proposed, but no platform is approved.
