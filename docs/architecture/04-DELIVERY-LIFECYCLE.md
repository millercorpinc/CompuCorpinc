# Delivery Lifecycle

## 1. Discovery

Capture:

- Business condition
- Desired outcome
- Stakeholders
- Existing systems
- Constraints
- Risk and compliance pressure
- User count and locations
- Internal capabilities
- Timeline and budget context

## 2. Assessment and architecture

Produce:

- Current-state summary
- Risks and gaps
- Options and tradeoffs
- Recommended target state
- Dependencies
- Transition approach
- Success measures

## 3. Scope and agreement

Define:

- Deliverables
- Assumptions
- Exclusions
- Customer responsibilities
- Acceptance criteria
- Timeline
- Price and payment
- Change process
- Recurring-service implications

## 4. Implementation

Manage:

- Work plan
- Environment access
- Configuration and build
- Migration
- Testing
- Communications
- Training
- Risks and decisions
- Partner activities

## 5. Acceptance and handoff

Deliver:

- Tested outcome
- Documentation
- Administrative ownership
- Training
- Decision log
- Risk register
- Support process
- Roadmap

## 6. Recurring governance

Review:

- Security and configuration
- Incidents and changes
- Documentation
- License and vendor position
- Automation health
- Roadmap
- Risks and exceptions
- Service quality and value
