# Company Operating Repository

This repository is the canonical operating and architecture system for the technology advisory, implementation, security, automation, and managed-governance company developed in the Startup project.

The legal name and public brand remain represented as **[COMPANY_NAME]** until approved.

Historical named-person assignments are no longer active. The repository defines required roles and functions without assuming any particular person is involved, an owner, or authorized.

## This is not a blank business brainstorm

The Startup project has already established the presumptive operating design:

- Advisory at the front
- Standardized implementation in the middle
- Recurring governance at the back
- Microsoft as the principal specialization
- Vendor-flexible integration across customer business systems
- A core market of approximately 10–150 employees, with a broader 1–200-user practical range
- Role-based commercial, strategy, technology, security, delivery, operations, finance, risk, and partner functions
- Structured use of distributors, partners, contractors, and independent professional firms
- Projects that lead into recurring governance and selective managed services
- Foundation Core at a 26–32-hour working baseline and $6,000–$9,000 range
- Full Foundation at a 46-hour controlled-environment baseline and $12,000–$18,000 range
- Pax8 as the presumptive launch distributor pathway, pending current diligence and contracting

Work in this repository should formalize, operationalize, validate, price, and execute that model rather than repeatedly reopening it.
