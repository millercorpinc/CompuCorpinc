# Open Questions

These are the genuine unresolved decisions. Do not re-list established business architecture as if it were unknown.

Historical named-person assignments are not active. Questions about current participants, owners, titles, assignments, and authority remain open.

## Name and legal identity

- What is the final legal and public company name?
- Are the name, domain, and marks available and acceptable?

## Ownership, participation, and governance

- Who will actually own and participate in the company?
- Which required functions will each current participant perform?
- What equity, vesting, capital contribution, voting, and compensation structure applies?
- What decisions require unanimity, majority, or function-level authority?
- What happens upon departure, disability, death, deadlock, or sale?
- Who owns pre-existing and newly created intellectual property?
- Who can bind the company contractually?
- Which functions will be employees, contractors, partners, or outsourced providers at launch?

## Legal, tax, finance, and risk

- Does the historical single-member LLC direction remain appropriate after current participation is known?
- When, if ever, should an S-corporation tax election be made?
- Which registrations are required?
- What insurance limits and coverages are appropriate?
- What contract terms and liability positions are acceptable?
- What startup capital and cash reserve are required?
- What financial controls and reporting cadence apply?

## Launch sequencing

- Does the company lead with a paid assessment, Foundation Core, Full Foundation, or an assessment-to-Foundation sequence?
- Is an initial vertical focus useful, or should launch remain horizontal within the target customer range?
- What evidence is required before publishing pricing?
- Who is the first pilot customer?
- What launch date and available capacity are realistic?

## Secure Workplace Foundation validation

- Which customer profiles qualify for Foundation Core versus Full Foundation?
- Which complexity factors require a paid assessment or custom scope?
- What gross-margin and contingency targets apply to the 26–32-hour and 46-hour baselines?
- What deposit, milestone, warranty, and change-order policies apply?
- Which Secure Score outcomes are reasonable by customer condition and licensing?
- What actual delivery evidence changes the baseline hours or scope?

## Managed-service boundary

- Which support requests are included?
- What hours and response targets apply?
- Is end-user help desk included, optional, or referred?
- What monitoring is included?
- What prerequisites are required before operational responsibility begins?
- What is excluded or separately project-priced?
- What is the minimum monthly fee?
- What customer conditions require termination or remediation?

## Commercial model

- What are the approved public launch prices?
- What gross-margin targets apply?
- Which costs are pass-through?
- What deposits, payment terms, milestone billing, and suspension rules apply?
- How are license-price changes handled?
- What discount and change-order authority applies?
- What warranty and included-support limits apply?

## Partnerships and distribution

- Which Microsoft partner pathway is appropriate under current program rules?
- Does Pax8 pass the current commercial, legal, security, support, billing, and integration diligence gate?
- What is the approved customer provisioning, billing, reconciliation, and escalation workflow through Pax8?

- Which initial specialist and independent partners are qualified?

## Internal operating stack

- Which specific products will support CRM, quoting, project/service management, documentation, accounting, reporting, secrets, and automation?

- What can be delayed until after the first pilots?

## Knowledge and solution system

- Which initial 20 solution opportunities should seed the Top 200 catalog?
- Which metadata is mandatory in repository records?
- When does real usage justify Dataverse or another dedicated application?
- Which workflows should be automated first?
- How will customer evidence be separated from reusable intellectual property?

## Execution and evidence

- Which metrics define successful first-quarter execution?
- How will actual effort be captured by work area?
