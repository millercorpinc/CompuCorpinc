# Decision Status

## How to use this file

The repository is not a blank business-design exercise. Established Startup-project directions are the presumptive operating design. Formal approval may still be required, but contributors should not reopen them without evidence of a contradiction, risk, or explicit owner request.

Historical named-person assignments are superseded. Use role functions only until current participants and authority are formally approved.
