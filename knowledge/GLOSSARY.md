# Glossary

**Advisory-led MSP:** A managed-services company that begins with strategy, architecture, and governance rather than commodity support.

**Canonical document:** The authoritative home of a fact, rule, or design.

**CSP:** Cloud Solution Provider; a Microsoft licensing and partner channel model.

**Distributor:** An intermediary that may provide license provisioning, billing, partner enablement, support escalation, and a catalog of adjacent products.

**Foundation project:** An initial project that establishes an approved, secure, documented baseline before recurring management.

**Fractional CIO/CTO:** Part-time executive technology leadership provided as a service.

**Managed governance:** Recurring oversight of configuration, security, documentation, vendors, changes, risk, and roadmap.

**Partner ecosystem:** External organizations used for specialized delivery, distribution, referral, independent review, or credentialed work.

**Productized service:** A repeatable service with a clear customer, outcome, scope, exclusions, delivery method, evidence, and pricing logic.

**Solution opportunity catalog:** A structured library of business problems and repeatable technology solutions ranked by expected impact and delivery effort.

**Superpowers:** A third-party agent-skills methodology that provides workflows such as brainstorming, planning, testing, debugging, and review.

**Working hypothesis:** A developed direction used for planning but not formally approved.
