# Brand and Concept History

Historical working names included:

- MillerCorp Managed Services
- Stable Systems
- Stable Advisory Group
- StableOps
- Solution Mill
- Radio, signal, relay, and frequency concepts
- Predicate Technology

The naming discussions consistently valued:

- Credibility
- Professional-services tone
- Trust and stability
- Technical depth
- Advisory capability
- A real-company sound
- Avoiding a repair-shop or commodity-help-desk identity

No final name is approved. Historical names must not be presented as current without a decision record.
