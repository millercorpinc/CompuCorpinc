# CompuCorp Brand Palette v1

Main orange: #E97B1F
Bright orange accent: #F39A42
Deep charcoal: #1F2A2D
Graphite: #4A545B
Warm gray: #EAE3DC
Off-white: #F7F4F1
White: #FFFFFF

Suggested usage:
- #E97B1F = primary identity accent
- #1F2A2D = headings, text, structure
- #EAE3DC = background neutral
- #F7F4F1 = light surface background
- #FFFFFF = clean white space
- #4A545B = secondary text and supportive UI
