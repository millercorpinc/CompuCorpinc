Working playbook

Deliver every engagement with evidence, secure ownership, and a clean handoff.

This workflow turns an approved scope into a governed customer outcome, then captures the learning needed to improve the next engagement.

Seven delivery stages

Control the work from intake through closeout.

1. Intake

  • Confirm signed scope and payment terms
  • Identify sponsor, contacts, and decision-makers
  • Confirm responsibilities, secure access, workspace, assumptions, risks, and dependencies

2. Plan

  • Break scope into workstreams and assign owners
  • Define milestones, test and acceptance
  • Identify partner activities and communication cadence

3. Design

  • Confirm current state and document target state
  • Review security and data requirements
  • Resolve material decisions and approve exceptions

4. Implement

  • Pilot high-risk changes
  • Use named administrative accounts and record changes
  • Maintain rollback considerations and escalate scope changes

5. Test

  • Test normal and exception paths
  • Verify security controls, monitoring, and administrator experience
  • Record defects and remediation

6. Accept and hand off

  • Obtain acceptance and deliver architecture/runbooks
  • Transfer ownership and record open risks
  • Define support, escalation, and recurring-service start or closeout

7. Learn

  • Compare planned and actual effort
  • Review margin and scope changes
  • Capture reusable assets, customer evidence, and expansion opportunities
