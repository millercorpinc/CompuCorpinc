Operations

Working playbook

Operate the company to the same security standard it recommends to customers.

The launch baseline establishes minimum controls for company identity, endpoints, information, secrets, response, partners, automation, and continuity.

Baseline controls

Security is a delivery prerequisite, not a later add-on.

Identity

  • Named accounts and MFA for all users
  • Separate privileged access and least privilege
  • Documented break-glass access and periodic review

Endpoints

  • Managed supported devices with encryption
  • Endpoint protection, patching, screen lock, and controlled local administration
  • Remote wipe where appropriate

Collaboration and data

  • Controlled external sharing and data classification
  • Customer separation, retention, and approved storage
  • No customer secrets in source repositories

Secrets

  • Approved password and secret manager
  • No shared credentials in documents or chat
  • Rotation, offboarding, and API-key inventory

Logging and response

  • Administrative logging and security alerting
  • Incident-response procedure and evidence preservation
  • Customer notification decision process

Vendors and partners

  • Security review proportionate to risk
  • Contract and data-processing review
  • Access expiration and minimum necessary customer data

Development and automation

  • Version control, testing, and secret scanning
  • Peer or senior review for high-risk changes
  • Change records, rollback, and recovery

Continuity

  • Backup critical company data
  • Recovery procedures and alternate communications
  • Dependency inventory and periodic testing
