Partner ecosystem

Stay accountable to the customer. Use partners for reach, distribution, and specialist capability.

The company is the customer’s strategic architect and accountable technology partner. Distributors, specialists, and referral partners extend delivery without becoming the primary value proposition or owning the customer relationship.

Microsoft program requirements, licensing, partner benefits, and distributor terms change. Follow the official links below and record the current evidence before any enrollment, purchase, or customer commitment.

Microsoft pathway

Join early; buy or pursue designations only when evidence supports it.

1. Establish the business

Before enrollment, confirm the legal entity, business email, address, authorized contact, and applicable registration details. Microsoft may verify company and employment information.

2. Enroll at no cost

Use the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program enrollment route to create the organization’s Partner Center account. Record who owns the tenant, partner profile, and security roles.

3. Build capability first

Use training, customer evidence, and delivery standards to decide whether paid benefits packages or future Solutions Partner designations are commercially justified.

How to interpret the 2026 guide

The guide supplied by the team shows a free program entry, then optional paid benefits packages. It lists Partner Launch Benefits at $350, Partner Success Core at $925, and Partner Success Expanded at $4,125 as of July 6, 2026.

Those figures are reference points only. Review Partner Center terms and current pricing before paying an annual fee. A Solutions Partner designation requires performance, skilling, and customer-success evidence; it is not a launch prerequisite.

Pax8 and distribution

Evaluate Pax8 as a candidate, not a foregone conclusion.

What a distributor should do

Support subscription provisioning, consolidated billing, licensing administration, renewals, technical escalation, enablement, and an operationally workable customer transition path.

Pax8 starting sequence

  1. Form the entity and clarify the launch offer.
  2. Start the partner application or request a marketplace demo.
  3. Ask about Microsoft licensing, billing, tax, support ownership, enablement, credit terms, and offboarding.
  4. Score Pax8 against another viable candidate.
  5. Pilot the selected process before scaling.

Decision guardrail

Do not choose a distributor solely on margin. Prioritize serviceability, customer experience, escalation quality, contract risk, security posture, and exit capability.

Questions for the first Pax8 call

  • Which Microsoft route, agreements, and regions are available to a new services firm?
  • Who owns tier-one customer communication and escalation?
  • How do onboarding, billing, renewal, suspension, and offboarding work?
  • What enablement, technical support, and professional services are available?
  • What customer data is held, and what is the transition process if the relationship ends?

Partner controls

Every material relationship needs a defined operating model.

Company retains

  • Customer strategy and discovery
  • Architecture and project ownership
  • Standards, documentation, and governance
  • Customer communication and scope control

Partners may supply

  • License provisioning and billing
  • Specialist security assessments
  • Independent audit or attestation
  • Field services, telecom, hardware, and deep engineering

Qualification checklist

  • Capability, credentials, independence, security, and insurance
  • Customer ownership, contracting, commercial terms, and escalation
  • Data handling, continuity risk, termination, and transition
