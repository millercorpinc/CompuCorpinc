# Launch Planning

1. Load `docs/launch/00-LAUNCH-PROGRAM.md`.
2. Identify current stage gate and blockers.
3. Separate founder decisions, professional-review tasks, build tasks, and validation experiments.
4. Order work by dependency and risk.
5. Define for each task:
   - Outcome
   - Owner role
   - Dependencies
   - Evidence of completion
   - Professional review needed
   - Decision record needed
6. Avoid polishing brand or software before founder and legal foundations are sufficiently clear.
7. Keep the backlog in `ops/launch-backlog.yaml` synchronized.
8. End with the next three executable tasks.
