# Solution Opportunity Analysis

For each opportunity:

1. Define the business process and owner.
2. Describe the current friction, cost, delay, or risk.
3. Identify systems by capability category.
4. Propose the simplest supportable solution pattern.
5. Evaluate security, data, change, and support.
6. Score expected impact and effort.
7. Explain the score; do not use arithmetic alone.
8. Define evidence needed.
9. Identify the commercial entry point.
10. Identify recurring governance or support.
11. Document using `templates/SOLUTION-OPPORTUNITY.md`.

Prefer high-impact, low-dependency, reusable opportunities.
