# Enterprise Architecture

Trace every design through:

1. Strategy and business outcome
2. Capability
3. Value stream or process
4. Information objects and ownership
5. People and partner roles
6. Application and integration support
7. Technology and security controls
8. Metrics and evidence
9. Transition plan

Rules:

- Business requirement before product.
- Microsoft is the principal specialization, not the only ecosystem.
- Prefer capability categories until a product decision is required.
- Identify systems of record and ownership.
- Include security, operations, support, cost, and exit.
- Separate current state, target state, and transition state.
- Record material choices in decision records.
- Use Mermaid when a diagram improves comprehension.
