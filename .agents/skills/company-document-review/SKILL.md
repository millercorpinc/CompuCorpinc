# Document Review

Review for:

- Alignment with `AGENTS.md`
- Correct status language
- Unapproved names or decisions
- Contradictions with canonical documents
- Duplicate canonical content
- Missing scope or exclusions
- Missing owner, evidence, or acceptance
- Missing security and operational considerations
- Vendor names used where capability categories are sufficient
- Partner and distributor omissions
- Legal, audit, tax, or insurance claims requiring qualification
- Broken relative links
- Unresolved placeholders other than intentional placeholders

Provide findings by severity and fix the document when authorized.
