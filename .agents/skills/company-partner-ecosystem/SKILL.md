# Partner Ecosystem Design

For each partner category or candidate evaluate:

- Customer need and role
- Why the capability should or should not be internal
- Credentials and independence
- Geographic and industry coverage
- Security and data handling
- Insurance and liability
- Contracting party
- Customer ownership
- Commercial terms
- Support and escalation
- Quality evidence
- Concentration and continuity risk
- Integration and operating overhead
- Exit and transition

Never select a distributor only on margin.

Distinguish:

- Distributor
- Reseller
- Referral partner
- Subcontractor
- Independent attestation or professional partner
- Technology vendor
- Strategic alliance
