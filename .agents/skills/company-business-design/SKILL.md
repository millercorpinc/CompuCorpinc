# Business Design

Use after the upstream brainstorming skill has established the problem and approval flow.

For each design:

1. State the decision to be made.
2. Identify current repository direction and unresolved assumptions.
3. Define success measures and constraints.
4. Present 2–3 materially different options.
5. Evaluate:
   - Customer value
   - Differentiation
   - Revenue and margin
   - Delivery feasibility
   - Founder fit
   - Risk
   - Reversibility
   - Partner dependence
6. Recommend an option without presenting it as approved.
7. Identify experiments or external advice needed.
8. After approval:
   - Create a decision record.
   - Update affected canonical documents.
   - Add implementation work to the backlog.
