# Service Productization

Start from a specific customer condition, not a technology list.

Produce:

- Ideal customer
- Trigger event
- Business outcome
- Qualification and disqualification rules
- Scope
- Exclusions
- Prerequisites
- Delivery stages
- Customer responsibilities
- Deliverables
- Acceptance evidence
- Risks and exception policy
- Required roles, skills, tools, and partners
- Cost drivers
- Pricing hypothesis
- Recurring attach
- Expansion path
- Pilot plan
- Metrics

Use `templates/SERVICE-BRIEF.md`.

Reject vague services that cannot define a buyer, outcome, boundary, and evidence.
